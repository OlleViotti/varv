Varv is a looper: what you record repeats in time with the click, layer by layer. Tap Record: one bar of count-in, then it records lap after lap, each into the next empty track, until you tap Record again. Hold Record to start or stop, hold Undo for what can be brought back; the record options are under Behaviour in Settings. A track's mic button records a sample to play on keys.

Headphones lag, so Varv moves each recording back to land on the beat. Measure the delay once under Audio in Settings (swipe left): Measure exactly for the headphones, Measure by tapping for the keys.

M mutes, S solos, ● records a lap, +● adds one on top, the mic records a sample, the keys open its pads. Hold a strip and slide for its volume.

Swipe right on a track to edit it, left for settings.

Undo takes back a recording, Capture last keeps the lap just played, the circle in the middle for tempo and click.

Overdub with Record

    
For the big Record button: on, a lap recorded onto a track that already has one plays on top of it; off, it replaces it. A track's own buttons choose for themselves.

Help

  

    
Space, R, Enter or → work like the Record button: tap to record, hold to start or stop. ← or Backspace undo. C capture last. K click. 1–9 mute a track, Shift+1–9 solo it. E edit, S settings, Esc back to play. P record the performance. A Bluetooth pedal that sends any of the record keys works as a record pedal.
